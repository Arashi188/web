Happy birthday, my sweet sweeet girl. ❤️

I pray Almighty Allah grants all your heart desires and blesses you beyond your expectations.

Every time I look at our friendship, I just smile, I was so sure our friendship would not be like what we had before. But I guess I was wrong😂 And somehow, despite everything, here we are. I still can’t believe that this is the first time I’m wishing you directly in the past two years, and honestly, I’m super grateful. I haven’t been able to wish you well directly. But somehow, you still found ways to make me smile.

I’ll never forget how you called me on your birthday last year despite the fact that it was YOUR birthday you called and said, “I know you’ve been looking for how to call me, so I decided to call you so you’ll wish me.” 😂 I smiled so hard because I’ve genuinely never seen someone that’s as crazy as you.

I bless the day you walked up to me in FSTC and said, “Do you live in Obanikoro? That’s my grandma’s place.” 😂 Who would have thought that one random conversation would lead to all of this?, You’ve been nothing but a sweet girl, inside and out, and I’m genuinely grateful for the gift of you.

For every path I’ve faced in LASU, I still feel like it would have been worse if you weren’t by my side. In my entire life, I’ve never seen anyone who would pause their sleep just to make sure her roommate isn’t losing her shit. 😂 You’d literally get up from your sleep just to make sure my sandals match my clothes, or just to reassure me that my outfit doesn’t look weird on me, even when I had the bright idea of writing jamb again you were there with me, encouraged me, gave me all the motivation I needed, and still followed me to write the exam. God blesss you 🫶

We’ve had our fair share of ups and downs in this friendship of a thing, but through everything, I’m genuinely, genuinely grateful that I get to do life with you.

You’re such a bright light, and I pray Almighty Allah never allows life( the shege we Dey face ) to steal that beautiful joy from you. I know our struggles, and that’s why I’m praying that whatever we lay our hands on shall be blessed.

I’m rooting for you My Taiwo❤️. I can’t wait to see you taking orders for Tee’s Treat, doing all the things you’ve always talked about, and becoming everything you’ve always wanted to be.

I’m rooting for you so much❤️.

I genuinely hope this new year of your life is softer, kinder and filled with so many beautiful things. I hope you get to experience the kind of happiness you deserve, and I hope Almighty Allah continues to guide you, protect you and bless everything that concerns you.

I love you soo, soo much, my baby. ❤️

Happy birthday once again, my sweet girl. 🤍
